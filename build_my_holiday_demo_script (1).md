# Build My Holiday: demo script

*Written for you to read aloud while recording.*

This version contains only what to say. Each section starts with a
one-line cue in italics describing what should be on screen. The wording
does not depend on exact clicks or numbers.

## 1. Introduction

*On screen: the home page.*

"Hi, we're presenting Build My Holiday.

Planning a holiday abroad means booking many separate things ---
flights, hotels, airport transfers, trains between cities and things to
do --- and they all have to fit together. If one part changes, the
others often stop fitting.

Build My Holiday solves this. You search once, and it builds a complete
holiday that fits your dates, your group and your budget. It keeps the
trip correct when you change anything, and lets you book everything with
one booking number."

## 2. The search

*On screen: filling in the search bar.*

"Let's plan a trip for the Menon family. They're two adults and a child
aged six, flying from Bengaluru to Japan on 10 April for six nights,
with a budget of three and a half lakh rupees.

The departure cities and countries come straight from the travel
catalog. We ask for each child's age, because it changes fares and the
rooms that fit the family. Under the search bar, the app already tells
us when we'll be back.

You can also type the whole trip in one line of plain English, and the
app fills the form for you. That's done with rules, not AI."

## 3. The package

*On screen: the trip page.*

"In a moment we have a complete holiday.

The app decided how to split the nights between cities. Here it's Tokyo
and Osaka, because a six-night trip gets two cities with at least two
nights in each.

On the right is the price summary: the total in rupees, how it splits
across flights, hotels, transfers and activities, and how much of the
budget is left.

Below is the day-by-day plan. The overnight flight leaves India the
evening before and lands on day one. Then the airport transfer to the
hotel, activities on each day, the bullet train to the next city, and
the flight home on the last day.

Before we see any package, it's checked against every travel rule:
flight times, transfer times, hotel nights, room sizes, seats left and
opening hours. A package that breaks even one rule is never shown."

## 4. Making changes

*On screen: changing a flight, a hotel and an activity.*

"Now let's make it ours.

When we change a flight, every option shows the price difference first.
After we pick one, the app re-checks the whole trip. If the new flight
lands at a different time, the airport transfer is moved automatically,
and we're told what changed.

We can change a hotel the same way. We can search, sort by price or
guest rating, or show only rooms with free cancellation.

We can add or remove activities. Only times that fit the day and the
opening hours are offered, so nothing overlaps.

If you'd rather split your nights between cities differently, you can
pick another plan here and the trip is rebuilt. Every change is saved as
a new version, so undo is always one click away."

## 5. Booking

*On screen: Book this trip, the details form, then the confirmation
page.*

"Happy with the trip, we book it. We enter the lead traveler's name and
email.

At this moment, seats and rooms are checked again and reserved, so the
same last seat can't be sold twice. We get one ten-digit booking number
for the whole holiday, plus a full confirmation with the itinerary and
the price paid. We can print it or download it."

## 6. Finding the booking

*On screen: Find a booking.*

"Anyone can open the booking later with that number. It's stored in the
database, so it still works after the app restarts. And if someone
enters a wrong number, the app clearly says the reservation was not
found."

## 7. Comparing three trips

*On screen: Compare 3 options and the three cards.*

"The app can also suggest three different trips for the same search: the
cheapest, the fastest --- the least time on planes and trains --- and
the best rated, with the most hotel stars that still fit your budget.
You pick one, and you can always go back and choose another."

## 8. When things don't fit

*On screen: a small budget, then an impossible request.*

"If no trip fits your budget, the app says so, shows the cheapest
possible trip, and suggests what to change.

If no trip is possible at all, for example when flights are full that
day, it explains why. And if you enter something that isn't valid, like
dates outside the season, you get a clear message right away."

## 9. Before you go

*On screen: the bottom of a trip page.*

"Finally, every trip comes with 'Before you go' tips: the usual weather
in each city, plug types, currency and local customs, and what to pack
for your activities. For example, shoes that slip off for temples, or
the dress code for a mosque. You can copy the list in one click."

## 10. Closing

*On screen: the home page or the confirmation.*

"Under the hood, it's a React frontend, a FastAPI backend and a MySQL
database, with over a hundred and fifty automated tests. Everything is
rule-based, with no AI.

That's Build My Holiday. One search, a complete holiday that stays
correct as you change it, and one booking number for everything. Thank
you."

*Timing:* At a calm pace this runs about 5--6 minutes. For 4 minutes,
